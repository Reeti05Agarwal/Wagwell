While you’re on the way

Bleeding

Press a clean cloth firmly on the wound and keep the pressure on. Don’t keep lifting it to check.

Suspected poisoning

Don’t make your pet vomit unless a vet tells you to. Bring the packet or a photo of what they ate.

Heatstroke

Move them to shade and wet their coat with cool — not ice-cold — water. Keep the car air-conditioned.
